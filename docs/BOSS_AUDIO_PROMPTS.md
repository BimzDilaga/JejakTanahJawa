# Prompt audio boss — Buto Ijo dan Headless Swordsman

Prompt musik berikut dapat dipakai pada generator musik. SFX dibuat sebagai klip terpisah, tanpa musik, agar serangan tetap terbaca. Headless Swordsman adalah sosok prajurit Belanda tanpa kepala dari rancangan fiksi game, bukan klaim tokoh sejarah.

## Musik Buto Ijo

```text
Instrumental boss battle music for a dark atmospheric pixel-art action game in a Javanese village at night. The boss is Buto Ijo, a massive green humanoid giant carrying a heavy iron sword. Strong Javanese gamelan identity: low gong and kempul cycles, weighty kendang patterns, dark slenthem pulses, restrained bonang and saron motifs, tense low rebab phrases. Approximately 92 BPM, heavy grounded rhythm with short gaps that leave room for sword impacts and parries. Menacing, earthy and ritual-like, powerful rather than frantic. Build tension through interlocking percussion and low metallic resonance, with a memorable short motif. A seamless 60–90 second gameplay loop, steady energy at both loop boundaries, no long intro or final ending. No lyrics, speech, EDM drop, electric-guitar lead or imitation of an existing soundtrack. Leave clear space in the midrange for combat SFX.
```

## Musik Headless Swordsman

```text
Instrumental supernatural boss battle music for a Javanese historical-fantasy pixel-art game at night. The boss is a headless Dutch colonial swordsman in a torn dark red uniform, wielding a saber, with purple smoke emerging from his collar. Combine a restrained ominous military snare pulse with Javanese kendang, deep gong, sparse kenong and cold bonang phrases. Approximately 104 BPM. Elegant measured sword-duel tension, hollow metallic resonance, dark low rebab textures, subtle wind-like sound design. The Javanese percussion remains central; the marching rhythm suggests the spectral soldier without turning into a triumphant parade. A memorable recurring motif, space for parry and saber-clash SFX, seamless 60–90 second instrumental gameplay loop. No lyrics, spoken narration, horse sounds, EDM drop, cheerful melody or copied soundtrack.
```

## Ambience malam pertemuan boss

```text
Quiet seamless Javanese rural night ambience for a side-scrolling game: soft leaf movement, distant insects, occasional sparse nocturnal bird calls, a very faint low hollow wind through trees. Tense and spacious with no jump-scare sting, human dialogue or music. Keep the center quiet so footsteps and NPC conversations are clear. A 30–45 second seamless environmental loop, no obvious start or ending.
```

## Prompt SFX Headless Swordsman

Generate each line as a separate isolated clip, no music or intelligible speech:

- **Step, 0.8 s:** One measured heavy leather military boot step on damp stone, a faint buckle rattle, crisp attack and short natural decay, spectral sword-duel game SFX, clean background.
- **Saber light, 0.7 s:** One precise fast saber cut, a narrow sharp air whistle and restrained steel vibration, elegant rather than a massive sword, dry isolated game SFX.
- **Saber heavy, 1.4 s:** A deliberate saber wind-up followed by one forceful sweeping cut, rising air rush and low metallic resonance, clear anticipation, clean isolated effect.
- **Parry, 1.0 s:** One sharp close clash of a small steel keris and a cavalry saber, clear bright blade ring and short controlled decay, no background ambience.
- **Spectral smoke, 2.5 s:** A brief inhaling-like supernatural smoke swirl from a headless collar, hollow wind and dark airy texture, no breathing person, words or music, isolated fantasy SFX.
- **Stance break, 1.8 s:** A military boot scrapes, the swordsman's knee hits stone and his saber rings weakly, brief disturbed spectral smoke, a readable isolated game cue.
- **Spawn, 3 s:** Hollow wind gathers, measured boots land once, a saber is drawn with a cold steel ring and spectral smoke disperses, no spoken voice or music.
- **Death, 3 s:** A saber drops on stone, a heavy coat and body collapse, hollow smoke releases in a fading gust; isolated supernatural defeat SFX with a clean ending.
